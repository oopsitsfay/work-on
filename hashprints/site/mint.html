01 Finishes

Same price.
Rarer with work.

Stop at the first valid hash, or keep searching. Every extra zero bit doubles the work, and enough of them turn a print into Gold Leaf or Holo Foil.

Common

93.75%

The run's required bits, up to 3 extra.

Gold Leaf

5.86%

4 to 7 extra zero bits. About 16× the work.

Holo Foil

0.39%

8 or more extra zero bits. About 256× the work.

02 Runs

Ten runs.
Rising difficulty.

3,330 public prints in 10 runs of 333. Difficulty starts at 16 zero bits and climbs one bit every two runs, so early prints are quicker to find.

03 How it works

Run. Found. Mint.

Nothing to install. The search runs in your browser, and the mint is one transaction at a flat price.

01 Run

Start a run

Connect your wallet, pick a target and start the miner. It tries nonces in your browser. Free, nothing on-chain.

02 Found

Find a hash

When a hash meets the run's target, the miner stops and shows the print it draws. Keep it, or run again.

03 Mint

Mint it

Confirm in your wallet. The hash is saved on-chain and becomes your print. Up to 2 per wallet.
